<p>I am a research lead at <a href="https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine">Luma AI</a>, currently leading pre-training and post-training for large-scale, generalizable world action models. Previously, I led pre-training and post-training for Luma’s <a href="https://lumalabs.ai/uni-1">UNI-1</a> multimodal image model, as well as follow-up research efforts for UNI storyboard (<a href="https://lumalabs.ai/news/introducing-luma-scenes">Luma Scenes</a>), manga, and super-resolution.</p>

<p>I was also the CTO and co-founder of <a href="https://apparate.ai">Apparate Labs</a> (acquired by Luma AI), where we trained a real-time video foundation model for generating highly realistic and expressive humans.</p>

<p>I received my PhD in computer science from Stanford University, co-advised by <a href="https://geometry.stanford.edu/member/guibas/">Leonidas Guibas</a> and <a href="https://stanford.edu/~gordonwz/">Gordon Wetzstein</a>. During my PhD, I interned at <a href="https://research.google/teams/perception">Google Research</a>, <a href="https://nv-tlabs.github.io">NVIDIA Research</a>, and <a href="https://research.adobe.com/careers/london/">Adobe Research</a>.</p>

<p>Previously, I worked at Google as a software engineer on Portrait Mode. I was also a Computer Science undergraduate and graduate student at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by <a href="https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/">Keenan Crane</a>.</p>

<p>Outside of work, I enjoy <a href="https://www.instagram.com/the_wizard_of_zoz/?hl=en">cooking</a>, badminton, swimming, boardgames, and <a href="https://www.last.fm/user/connorzl">music</a> (long-time swiftie). Also a fan of traveling and sports – go Ravens!</p>
